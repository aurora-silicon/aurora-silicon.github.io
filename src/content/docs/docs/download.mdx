## Omarchy Mac

Omarchy is our flagship distribution, built together with the Omarchy team for Apple Silicon Macs. Omacom publishes the installer on GitHub.

<LinkButton href={omarchyUrl} icon="external">Omarchy Mac releases</LinkButton>

## Aurora Linux

The Aurora Linux installer sets up the distribution of your choice: Fedora, Arch Linux or NixOS. [Installer](/docs/userland/installer/) explains what it does and what it copies from your macOS.

It is alpha software. Builds are on GitHub releases and are not signed or notarized yet. Expect rough edges, and keep a backup of your Mac.

<LinkButton href={auroraInstallerUrl} icon="external" variant="secondary">Aurora Linux installer releases</LinkButton>
