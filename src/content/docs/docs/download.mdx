## Omarchy Mac

Omarchy is our flagship distribution, built together with the Omarchy team for Apple Silicon Macs. Omacom publishes the installer on GitHub.

## Aurora Linux

The Aurora Linux installer sets up the distribution of your choice: Fedora, Arch Linux or NixOS. [Installer](/docs/userland/installer/) explains what it does and what it copies from your macOS.

Alpha software. Builds are not signed or notarized yet. Keep a backup.
