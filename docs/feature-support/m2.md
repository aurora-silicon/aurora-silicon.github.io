## Support

Bring-up on this generation happens on the M2 Pro primary target, and support
states are now recorded for it — select `j414s` in the machines table for the
per-feature detail. These Windows results apply to this board alone; the other
machines still need their own testing.

### MacBook Pro 14-inch, M2 Pro — `j414s`

**Working.** The internal SSD controller; Wi-Fi and Bluetooth; keyboard and
trackpad with full gesture support and keyboard backlight; USB 3, including
Ethernet with Realtek's arm64 driver installed; and SMC battery reporting,
battery health reporting and fan control. The processor reports fully to
Device Manager and Task Manager — clock speed, cpufreq for adjusting it, and
the different power modes — and P/E core scheduling works natively. The power
button is bound to ++win+l++ (a long-press shutdown prompt is a TODO; it needs
a user-land client). On graphics, the Honeykrisp Vulkan port runs on Windows
and DXVK runs over it.

**In progress.** WDDM 2.1 is the crucial and most difficult piece — it is what
lets DWM and all apps compose through the DX11/Vulkan driver — and it is
coming together. 120 Hz, VRR and HDR for the ProMotion display are in
progress, as is the SD card reader.

**Left to do.** The entire media stack — speakers, microphone, webcam. USB4 /
Thunderbolt and HDMI. Deep sleep, which matters most for real daily use as a
portable and should be straightforward.
[Touch ID](../research/security/touch-id.md), where the driver is slowly
coming along — SEPOS is difficult to poke around. Nested virtualization for
WSL and VMs.

!!! warning "One machine recorded"

    These states come from bring-up on `j414s` alone. Nothing on this page is
    a support claim for the other eleven machines.

## Linux bring-up

The M2 Linux tree has the main platform, storage, graphics and display paths in place. The remaining work depends on the machine: Mac Pro Bluetooth still needs checking, and a working driver does not by itself make a complete installation.

The [MacBook Pro thread](https://github.com/aurora-silicon/linux/discussions/106) records Touch ID work on `j414c` and separate work on `j414s`. It also records USB peripherals through a Thunderbolt hub on `j416s`; display tunnels and resume have their own limits. The target branch matters — a change merged into `feat/sep` is not necessarily in `aurora-wip`.

These Linux notes are from **1 October 2026**. They are separate from the Windows
states above. The [support overview](overview.md#reading-a-support-state) explains
how code, hardware results and unfinished work are recorded.
