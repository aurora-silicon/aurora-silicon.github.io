# Artwork

The Aurora Silicon mark is a four-pane window with a bite taken from the
upper-right pane, running warm orange to deep blue — Windows and Apple silicon
in one shape.

!!! note "No SVG, and an honest reason why"

    The mark is continuous-tone artwork — glossy shading and soft gradients
    across every pane, roughly 200,000 distinct colours. Automatic tracing
    would either flatten it to a silhouette, losing the identity entirely, or
    emit tens of thousands of paths approximating gradients: larger than the
    PNG and worse looking.

    A genuine vector version has to be **drawn**, not converted. Until someone
    does that, the 1024px PNG is the master and the smaller sizes are derived
    from it.

## Using the mark

**You may use it to talk about Aurora Silicon.** Articles, videos, blog posts,
talks, forum threads, packaging that mentions the project, community materials —
no permission needed, no attribution required. If you are covering the project,
please do use it; that is what it is for.

**Please do not:**

- Use it as the identity of a **different** project, product, or service
- Use it in a way that implies Aurora Silicon endorses, maintains, or is
  affiliated with something it is not
- Redraw, recolour, stretch or add to the mark and continue to present it as the
  Aurora Silicon mark — scale it freely, but keep its proportions and colours
- Use it as your own trademark or brand

The short version: **use it to point at us, not to stand in for you.**

## Related marks

The mark deliberately echoes both Windows and Apple imagery, because that is
what the project is about. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced
by Microsoft or Apple, and it should never be presented as either company's
mark or as evidence of their involvement.

Aurora Silicon is likewise **not** affiliated with Asahi Linux — see the
[FAQ](faq.md). Please do not combine this mark with Asahi's in a way that
suggests otherwise.
